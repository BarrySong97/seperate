Seperate was Chinese-only until now. From this release the whole app is available in English and Chinese, and it follows your Mac's language unless you pick one yourself.

### English and Chinese, your choice

![Seperate menu › Language](mock:language-menu)

Menus, dialogs, the sidebar, the inbox, the command palette and notifications are all translated. By default Seperate uses Chinese when your Mac's preferred language is Chinese and English otherwise. To choose yourself, open **Seperate › Language** and pick System Default, 中文 or English. Seperate offers to restart so the change takes effect; your projects, workspaces and session names stay exactly as they are.

### An installer in your language

The disk image now opens with "Install Seperate" (shown as "安装 Seperate" on a Chinese Mac), and the installer window follows your system language. The background explains the steps in both languages.

#### Improvements

- Notifications from Claude Code and Codex (questions, plans waiting for review) use the same language as the app
- Times read naturally in English: "5m", "3h", "yesterday", "Sep 12"
- The website is now in English too, with Chinese at seperate.vercel.app/zh
